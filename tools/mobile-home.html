Before you start

Is this real trading?

No. The paper desk uses simulated balances and orders. It cannot deposit, withdraw or trade your real money.

Do I need a wallet?

You can use the paper-trading desk and calculators without connecting a wallet or making a deposit.

Can a simulation predict my returns?

No. Real execution, liquidity, costs and behaviour can differ. Use the exercises to understand mechanics, not as a promise of profit.
