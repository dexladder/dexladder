## What is this?

DexLadder is a **client-side crypto market simulator and academy** — one self-contained HTML file that runs in your browser (or as an installed PWA / Android app), online or offline:

- 📈 **Real-time markets** — 500+ coins, heatmap, screeners, Fear & Greed, DEX trends
- 💹 **Trading terminal** — candles + indicators, order book, TP/SL, options overlays
- 💼 **Portfolio** — paper balances, DCA & grid bots, journal, analytics, tax estimate
- 🤝 **P2P desk** — the full escrow lifecycle, every counterparty a bot
- 🔍 **On-chain explorer** — trace real transactions across 15 chains
- 🎓 **Academy** — 12 visual lessons, playable sims, films, glossary, and a certificate signed with real SHA-256 + secp256k1 on your device

**No accounts. No ads. No trackers. No server. No real money — ever.**

## Principles

1. **No real money — ever.** Paper balances only. No deposits, withdrawals, tokens or ICOs.
2. **No company, no VC.** Independent educational project, built anonymously and gifted to the community.
3. **Client-side or it doesn't ship.** Your data lives in your browser's localStorage and nowhere else.
4. **Real data, honest labels.** Live prices from public APIs; every simulated surface carries a "Simulated" badge.
5. **Don't trust — verify.** View Source. Watch the network tab. Pull the plug and it keeps working.

## Verify it yourself

- **View Source** — the whole app is one readable file.
- **DevTools → Network** — only public market-data APIs; no telemetry exists.
- **Go offline** — the app keeps running on cached data and says so.
- **Application → Local Storage** — your data, all of it, deletable in one click.
